What to prioritize in a bathroom fixture upgrade

Most bathroom renovation budgets get spent on the wrong line items first. Tubs and vanities get picked for looks in a showroom, while the hardware that actually fails first — towel bars, toilet paper holders, faucet finishes — gets an afterthought budget. The picks below are grouped so you can build a coordinated matte black or brass hardware set alongside the bigger fixtures, without mismatched finishes creeping in room to room.

Two things matter more than most buying guides mention: standard US sizing (so nothing needs custom framing or rough-in work) and finish durability in a humid environment, where chrome and cheap zinc plating fail years before solid brass does.
